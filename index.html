SB & Sons – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how SB & Sons Transport Services Limited ("we", "us") uses personal data in the SB & Sons iOS app. The app is a private business tool for arranging and carrying out kitchen deliveries for our trade customers, Howdens Joinery and Cliftons depots. It is not available to the public.

Who we are

SB & Sons Transport Services Limited is a limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 14311211, owned and directed by Stephen Brierley.

Registered office: 277 Stockport Road, Ashton-under-Lyne, OL7 0NT, United Kingdom. Trading address: 12 Bottoms Fold, Mossley, Ashton-under-Lyne, OL5 9NH, United Kingdom.

We are the data controller for the data in the app.

Privacy contact: sbtransportservices@hotmail.com

Who uses the app

Our admin staff, our drivers, and staff at our customers' depots. Accounts are created by our admin; you sign in with your work email and a password.

What data we use

  • App users (staff, drivers, depot staff): name, work email, phone number, role, depot or company, and sign-in details (passwords are stored securely in hashed form by our hosting provider).
  • Delivery customers: name, delivery address and postcode, phone number, and any delivery notes, as supplied by the depot that books the delivery.
  • Delivery records: time slot (First Drop, 9am–12pm, 12–5pm), status updates, assigned driver, and delivery history.
  • Proof-of-delivery photos: photos a driver takes or chooses using the phone's camera or photo library when completing a delivery. The app only accesses the camera/photos when you choose to add a photo.
  • Location: the app does not collect or track your device's location. Map routes are worked out from the depot and delivery addresses, not from the driver's phone.

Route and postcode lookups

To show the road route, miles and drive time, depot and delivery postcodes or map coordinates are sent to:

  • postcodes.io – to convert postcodes into map coordinates;
  • OpenRouteService and/or OSRM (project-osrm.org) – to calculate road routes.

Only postcodes/coordinates are sent – no names or phone numbers.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis (UK GDPR)

  • To arrange, assign and complete deliveries and keep proof of delivery – legitimate interests in running our delivery business and fulfilling our contracts with our trade customers.
  • To give staff and depot users accounts – legitimate interests (and, for our own staff, our employment/engagement relationship).
  • To deal with queries, complaints or disputes about deliveries – legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties – legal obligation.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase – hosts our database, sign-in and file storage in its London (UK) region.
  • Route and postcode services listed above, for postcode/coordinate lookups only.
  • Howdens / Cliftons – the depot that booked a delivery can see its status and proof of delivery.
  • Authorities or advisers where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data, show adverts, or use tracking or analytics for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Proof-of-delivery photos and delivery details (customer names, addresses, phone numbers, notes, status notes and saved routes): deleted automatically 60 days after the delivery date.
  • Basic job history: after 60 days we keep only the delivery postcode, date, depot and time slot (with the job status, assigned driver and mileage), so we can keep job history and invoice our customers.
  • Staff and depot accounts: deleted when the person leaves or no longer needs access.
  • Accounting records: as long as the law requires (usually 6 years).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access is limited by role (for example, drivers only see deliveries assigned to them), and sign-in details are stored securely on your device.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or for a copy to move elsewhere. Email us at the address above; we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it first.

Children

The app is for business use only and is not aimed at anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update this page if things change and show the new effective date above.
